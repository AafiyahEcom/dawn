01 — Brand Foundation

The Story Behind the Name

Our Mission

Bare & Bloom exists for women in their most transformative season. We design clothing that holds you gently — through the swell of pregnancy, the tenderness of nursing, and the quiet becoming of postpartum. Every piece is made so you feel beautiful without trying to.

Our Vision

To be the brand mothers reach for instinctively — trusted for its softness, loved for its honesty. A brand that sees the full woman: her strength, her vulnerability, her bloom.

Bare

The intimacy of skin-to-skin. The openness of a new mother. The honesty of a body that has grown and changed and is worthy of love exactly as it is. Bare is our promise of authenticity.

Bloom

The physical and emotional transformation of becoming a mother. The quiet unfolding. The beauty of something new taking shape. Bloom is our promise of growth — and of celebrating every stage of it.

05 — Brand Voice

How We Speak

Tender & Honest

We speak to mothers like a trusted friend — warmly, without performance. We acknowledge that this season is hard AND beautiful, and we never pretend otherwise.

Quietly Confident

We don't shout. We don't use pushy language. We believe in our products and let the quality speak. Our tone is assured, not aggressive.

Poetic, Not Flowery

We choose words carefully. One beautiful sentence over three ordinary ones. We write copy that mothers save and reread — not copy they scroll past.

Sample Copy

"For the version of you that is soft in places you didn't expect,
and stronger than you ever imagined."

Our nursing bras are designed for the moments between moments — the 3am feeds, the quiet afternoons, the days when comfort isn't a luxury, it's a necessity. Bare & Bloom. Made for the full woman.

06 — Imagery Direction

Visual World

Natural light · warm tones
Outdoor · soft greens
Intimate · close crop
Quiet moments · lifestyle

Photography Style

Warm, natural light. Real women in real moments. No harsh shadows, no over-retouching. Backgrounds are neutral — linen, wood, soft plaster. Models of diverse body types and skin tones at all stages of pregnancy and postpartum.

Art Direction

Think: morning light through sheer curtains. A mother looking out a window. Soft botanicals. Unhurried. Never overly styled or aspirational to the point of alienation. Beautiful, but real.

07 — Brand Rules

Do & Don't

We Do

  • Use soft, warm, earthy tones always
  • Speak to the full experience of motherhood
  • Show real bodies at every stage
  • Let white space breathe in our layouts
  • Use Cormorant Garamond for all display text
  • Write copy that is poetic and precise
  • Pair terracotta as our primary CTA colour
  • Use sage as a complementary, grounding accent
  • Celebrate vulnerability and strength equally

We Don't

  • Use cold, clinical whites or harsh contrast
  • Use generic sans-serif fonts (Arial, Roboto)
  • Use purple, neon, or highly saturated colours
  • Write pushy, sales-heavy copy
  • Over-retouch or over-style photography
  • Use baby motifs or overly cutesy imagery
  • Crowd layouts — we breathe, always
  • Shout with all-caps headlines at large scale
  • Position motherhood as only soft — it's fierce too
